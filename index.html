01 — About

Coral Yiwei Qin

I am a scholar of language assessment and education policy, with a particular focus on how evaluation practices shape access, opportunity, and identity for immigrants and multilingual learners navigating high-stakes systems.

My doctoral dissertation reconceptualizes language testing for immigration as a technology of migration governance. Combining policy archaeology with participatory co-analysis involving Chinese immigrants in Canada, I examine how language tests construct the boundaries of the "ideal immigrant" and shape migrants' access to mobility, belonging, and opportunity. The study centers the lived experiences of those navigating Canada's immigration language requirements through qualitative methodologies including focus groups and participatory co-analysis.

I have taught at the graduate level at the University of Ottawa, where my courses have addressed second language teaching trends and the intersection of technology, languages, and education. I have also contributed to large-scale international editorial projects with Wiley and Routledge, and presented research across North America, Europe, and Asia.

I speak Mandarin, Cantonese, English, and basic Portuguese, and I remain endlessly curious about the politics of language.

03 — Teaching

Teaching & Instruction

My teaching is grounded in critical inquiry and a commitment to connecting theory with practice. I create space for students to engage with contested ideas, develop scholarly voices, and think carefully about the social consequences of language and assessment.
